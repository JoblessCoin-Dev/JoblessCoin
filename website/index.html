    FAQ

    Questions, answered straight.

    What is JoblessCoin?

    A memecoin about breaking out of the 9 to 5 loop. The ring in our logo is the daily grind. The J is you, leaving it. That's the whole idea. No hidden utility, no promises.

    What is JOB?

    JOB is the ticker, and the token itself. A Solana token with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000.

    Why Solana?

    Fast and cheap, and it has the tools to prove everything onchain, like locked liquidity and vesting. That's what makes the proof terminal above possible.

    What is Token-2022?

    Solana's newer token standard. It lets a token carry its own name and logo onchain. It also supports extra powers like transfer fees or freezing wallets. JOB uses none of them, and the terminal checks that live.

    Is this real money?

    No, not right now. JOB only exists on Devnet, Solana's test network. Devnet tokens have zero value and can't be bought or sold for real money.

    What is Devnet?

    A practice copy of Solana. Same software, free test coins. Builders use it to try everything out before touching real money. We're doing a full rehearsal there first.

    What is the total supply?

    1,000,000,000 JOB. The plan is 90% liquidity, 5% community and 5% development. On Devnet that split is already done and verified.

    Can more JOB be created?

    No. Minting is switched off forever. Nobody can ever make more, including us. The terminal checks it live every time you load this page.

    Where is the official token address?

    Only this one: EfFBNJSuLDJLt2bU6QuxUriq5nhHgoBMYksRhWxH4tRi. It's also in our GitHub repo. Anything else is not JOB.

    How do I avoid fake JOB tokens?

    Anyone can make a token called JOB. Only the address counts. Compare every character, use the checker on this page and ignore anyone who DMs you with a deal.

    Does the website ever ask me to connect my wallet?

    Never. We will never ask you to connect your wallet on this website. No seed phrases, no private keys, no claims, no airdrops. If a site asks for any of that, close it.
